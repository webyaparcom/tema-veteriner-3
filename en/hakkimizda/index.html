Care checklist

Six steps at every visit

Steps are ticked as you scroll; the same list is used at all three branches. Content is fictional and does not replace a veterinary examination.

  1. check
    pets

    01

    A calm welcome

    Cats and dogs wait in separate areas; carriers stay closed while you wait.

  2. check
    scale

    02

    Weighing

    Weight is recorded at every visit; medicine doses depend on it.

  3. check
    stethoscope

    03

    General examination

    Eyes, ears, mouth, skin and heart sounds are checked.

  4. check
    vaccines

    04

    Vaccination card

    Missing vaccines and parasite treatments are noted on the card.

  5. check
    edit_note

    05

    Written care note

    Feeding and medicine use are explained in a short note.

  6. check
    notifications

    06

    Reminder

    A message is sent a week before the next vaccine is due.
